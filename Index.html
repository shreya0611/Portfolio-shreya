Questions people ask

Can’t find yours? Send me a message and I’ll reply.

What kinds of projects excite you most?

Ones where good design changes how something feels to use: websites, apps and products that combine interface, brand and a physical or spatial idea.

Do you do branding as well as UI/UX?

Yes. I design logos, colour and type systems, posters and social creatives alongside product interfaces, so the brand and the product look like one thing.

Are you open to freelance or collaboration?

Yes. Use the form below to tell me about your project, timeline and what you need.

Which tools do you use?

Figma for interface work. Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign and Canva for graphics. Fusion 360 and KeyShot for 3D products, and ShapesXR for XR layouts.

How do you start a new project?

I research the people and the problem, sketch flows and wireframes, prototype in Figma, test, then refine before handing over.
